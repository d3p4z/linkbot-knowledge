## Key Points
- Conway's Law: Organizations' communication structures dictate system design.
- Hyrum's Law: All observable behaviors of a system will eventually be depended upon.
- The Boy Scout Rule: Leave code better than you found it.
- YAGNI: Don't add functionality until it is necessary.
- Brooks's Law: Adding people to a late project makes it later.
- The Law of Leaky Abstractions: Abstractions are imperfect and can lead to unexpected issues.
- Postel's Law: Be conservative in what you send, liberal in what you accept.
- Broken Windows Theory: Unrepaired flaws lead to more flaws.
