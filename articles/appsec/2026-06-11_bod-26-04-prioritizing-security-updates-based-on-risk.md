# BOD 26-04: Prioritizing Security Updates Based on Risk

## What This Is
This is a Binding Operational Directive (BOD) from CISA that mandates federal agencies prioritize security updates based on risk, moving away from a one-size-fits-all approach to vulnerability remediation.

## Why It Matters
This directive is crucial for application security as it enforces a more efficient and effective strategy for managing vulnerabilities, focusing resources on the most critical threats and reducing the attack surface of federal systems.

## Key Points
- Agencies must update vulnerability management policies to align with risk-based prioritization.
- Remediation timelines are determined by factors like asset exposure, exploit automation, and technical impact, with a focus on Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEVs).
- Automated reporting of vulnerability status via the CDM Dashboard is required.
- This directive supersedes previous BODs (19-02, 22-01) and consolidates vulnerability remediation guidelines.
- Phased implementation includes immediate policy review, 60-day process updates, and 180-day remediation timelines.
