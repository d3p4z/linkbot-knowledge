# Everyone wants to be AI-pilled. Most Companies Are Still Level 1

## What This Is
This article outlines a six-level framework (L0-L5) for assessing a company's AI maturity, moving beyond a binary 'AI-pilled' status to evaluate the depth and breadth of AI integration into an organization's operations and capabilities.

## Why It Matters
Understanding an organization's AI maturity is crucial for application security as it informs the potential attack surface, the complexity of AI-integrated systems, and the security practices needed to manage AI-driven workflows and data.

## Key Points
- Companies are often categorized as 'AI-pilled' or not, but true AI maturity exists on a spectrum.
- The framework assesses AI capabilities based on what AI can 'see' and 'do', who can 'extend the system', and how the 'organization has changed'.
- Levels range from L0 (AI as theater) to L5 (virtually self-driving organization), with increasing levels of autonomy, integration, and systemic change.
- Moving up the levels requires AI to move from personal productivity tools to core organizational infrastructure and autonomous decision-making.
