# Exploring the Emerging Threats of the Agent Skill Ecosystem

## TLDR
- **76 malicious AI agent skills** were confirmed, featuring credential theft, backdoors, and data exfiltration.
- **13.4% of all analyzed skills** have critical security issues, with 8 malicious ones still public.
- **Automated security analysis is essential** due to scale, complexity, novel attack vectors (like prompt injection), and high-value targets accessed by AI agents.
- **Prompt injection and malicious code** are the most prevalent threats, with 91% of confirmed malicious skills using both.
- **Skills fetching third-party content** pose a significant risk for indirect prompt injection, allowing attackers to poison data sources.
- **Hardcoded secrets are found in 34 skills**, including accidentally leaked keys and deliberately embedded tokens for malicious purposes.
- **Marketplaces like clawhub.ai show higher risk rates** compared to curated lists like skills.sh's top 100.
